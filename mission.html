Our Story

For over 30 years, Kalmont Mountain Rescue has been a trusted presence in the rugged and unpredictable terrain of the Kalmont Mountain Range. Our mission is to keep adventurers safe—whether you're hiking forested trails, scaling icy cliffs, or carving fresh tracks through backcountry snow. We offer real-time safety updates, expert rescue services, and essential resources to help you plan and enjoy your outdoor journey with confidence.

Our dedicated team is made up of highly trained rescue professionals, paramedics, wilderness guides, and passionate volunteers. With expertise in rapid response, technical search and rescue, and wilderness first aid, we're ready to act swiftly in any emergency. We know these mountains inside and out—and we're here to ensure your adventure is not only thrilling but also as safe as possible.

Our Mission

At Kalmont Mountain Rescue, our mission is to keep adventurers safe through rapid response, expert rescue operations, and up-to-date safety information. Whether preventing accidents or providing emergency assistance, we are dedicated to protecting everyone who explores Kalmont’s mountains.
